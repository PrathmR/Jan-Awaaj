# Terms of Use (MVP Draft)

## 1. Acceptance
By using the app and submitting complaints or interacting with the public feed, you agree to these Terms.

## 2. Acceptable use
You agree not to submit:
- fraudulent or abusive content
- personally identifying information (PII) in the description or photo
- content intended to harm or harass individuals/groups

Authorities may update complaint status and add notes/proof related to resolution.

## 3. Public feed and community interactions
If you enable “Share publicly”, your complaint may appear in the community feed near a location radius.
Feed items are privacy-first and should not show user identity.

Votes and comments are intended for community validation and feedback.

## 4. Disclaimer
This MVP is a prototype and may contain errors. The system provides updates based on submitted and processed data.
The organization running the service is not liable for losses resulting from incorrect submissions or delayed updates.

## 5. Changes to terms
We may update these Terms as the product evolves. Continued use after updates means acceptance.
